About us

Two decades of supplying the people who build in Sangareddy.

A trusted name in local building supply

Sri Laxmi Prasanna Traders has been supplying building materials from Ootla for more than 20 years. We started by serving local builders and homeowners, and we have grown by doing the same thing every day: stock what the job needs, quote honestly and deliver what we promised.

Today we carry steel, cement, hardware, electricals and the full range of home construction materials, so customers can complete a purchase in one place instead of visiting several shops.
